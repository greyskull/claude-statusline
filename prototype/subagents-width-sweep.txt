YAS_LABELS=true turns on the column-label overlay baked into the section's
separator_dim border row -- that's why the border dashes above each section
read as fused words ("input sess/day", "loc read / written", ...): the labels
are stamped directly onto the border characters at their anchor columns, not
printed on a separate row. This is the real renderer behaviour for
`[layout] labels = true` / `YAS_LABELS=true`, not a demo-harness quirk.

Each render's own top-border row spans the full terminal width requested
(visible column count verified to match COLUMNS for each snapshot below).
